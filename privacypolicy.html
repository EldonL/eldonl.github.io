By accessing or using the Site, Games, or Services, or by voluntarily providing personal information to us, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. COLLECTION AND USE OF YOUR INFORMATION

Confused Pizza does not directly collect or store personal information from users through the Games. The Games do not require users to create a Confused Pizza account or provide personal information in order to play.

If you voluntarily contact Confused Pizza for technical support, you may provide personal information, such as your email address or information about your device or technical issue. We use this information only to respond to your request and provide technical support.

The Games may be distributed through third-party platforms, including Meta Quest. These platforms may independently collect or process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms. Confused Pizza does not control or have access to personal information independently collected by these third-party platforms.

If our data collection or privacy practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect those changes.

DELETION OF YOUR DATA

For general users, Confused Pizza does not maintain personal information collected through gameplay, and therefore there is generally no gameplay-related personal information to delete.

If you voluntarily provide personal information to Confused Pizza for technical support, you may contact us to request deletion of that information. Support information will be deleted after the technical issue has been resolved, unless it is necessary to retain the information for an ongoing support matter or as required or permitted by law.

You can contact us using the information provided under “CONTACT INFORMATION” to request deletion of information you have provided to us.
