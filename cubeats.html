About

Cubeats is a VR rhythm puzzle game where the player acts as a music engineer. The goal is to rebuild a target musical sequence using physical, tactile objects instead of traditional graphs or charts. We intentionally steered away from the common futuristic look of many VR rhythm games. Our core inspiration comes from nostalgia for 90s analog technology, specifically the satisfyingly clunky experience of using cassettes and recorders to create simple, physical tracks.

Cubeats started off as a hackathon in the Meta Horizon Start Developer Competition which can be found on Devpost here. Using Unity and Meta hand tracking, we have transitioned from a prototype into a more robust, stable game, ready for wider testing, expansion, and exhibition
